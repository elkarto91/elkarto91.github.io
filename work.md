## Enterprise Platforms

<div class="work-entry">
  <div class="section-label">Compliance · Blockchain · SAP Integration</div>
  <h3>TaxChain / PID — EU VAT Compliance</h3>
  <div class="meta">Enterprise Architect & Product Lead · KrypC · 2022–Present</div>
  <span class="pill pill-purple">Hyperledger Fabric</span>
  <span class="pill pill-cyan">SAP S/4HANA</span>
  <span class="pill pill-pink">Compliance</span>
  <span class="pill pill-gold">Blockchain</span>

  <p class="problem" style="margin-top:1rem;"><strong>The problem:</strong> VAT fraud in EU cross-border transactions caused significant revenue leakage and years of audit friction. Existing systems couldn't prove delivery without exposing commercial confidentiality. Regulators needed evidence. Enterprises needed privacy. Nobody had built the middle.</p>

  <p><strong>What was built:</strong> Hyperledger Fabric-anchored evidence management capturing documents, digital signatures, and cryptographic hashes. SAP S/4HANA adapters for seamless ERP integration with no rip-and-replace. Regulator read-access portals with role-based controls. Auditor persona workflows with full chain-of-custody and sequence diagrams.</p>

  <div class="outcome">
    <strong>Impact:</strong> 80%+ reduction in VAT fraud and disputes. Regulator-ready audit trails meeting EU compliance requirements. Best Blockchain Implementation — European Tax Summit 2022.
  </div>
</div>

<div class="work-entry gold">
  <div class="section-label">Open Source · AI Governance · Founder</div>
  <h3>Neuralic — Open AI Governance & Observability</h3>
  <div class="meta">Founder / Chief Architect · 2023–Present</div>
  <span class="pill pill-gold">Open Source</span>
  <span class="pill pill-cyan">Policy-as-Code</span>
  <span class="pill pill-purple">Lineage Graphs</span>
  <span class="pill pill-pink">Red-Team Harnesses</span>

  <p class="problem" style="margin-top:1rem;">Enterprises were deploying AI and LLM systems with no trustworthy oversight mechanisms. Compliance risks, operational blind spots, no lineage, no explainability, no audit trail. When something went wrong, there was no chain of evidence. The governance frameworks that existed were either too theoretical or didn't ship as code.</p>

  <p>Neuralic is the platform I built to solve that: policy stores for rule management, lineage graphs for decision traceability, prompt/response capture, explainability interfaces with attribution models, red-team testing harnesses, and audit-ready documentation bundles.</p>

  <p>It's open source because this needs to become an industry standard — not a moat.</p>

  <div class="outcome">
    <strong>Status:</strong> Live and in active development. Informing AI control frameworks across multiple organisations. <a href="https://github.com/elkarto91">View on GitHub →</a>
  </div>
