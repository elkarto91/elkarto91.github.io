## How I Engage

**Advisory Sessions**
90-minute focused sessions on a specific architecture or product challenge. Good for: decision reviews, design critiques, roadmap pressure-testing, second opinions on critical technical choices.

**Workshops**
Half-day or full-day working sessions. Good for: capability mapping exercises, OKR alignment, integration pattern design, architecture education for cross-functional teams.

**Architecture Audits**
Structured review of existing systems against your stated goals. Produces a written assessment with prioritised recommendations, risk identification, and a clear path forward. No slide decks padded with methodology diagrams.

**Retained Advisory**
Monthly engagement for ongoing guidance. Good for: sustained architectural oversight, CTO/VP-level support, board-facing technology narrative, long-horizon transformation programmes.
